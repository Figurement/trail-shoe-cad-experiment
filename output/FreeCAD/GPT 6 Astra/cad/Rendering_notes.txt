OPEN / RENDER
Open the FCStd file in FreeCAD. The construction group is intentionally hidden.
The numbered material groups separate upper panels, overlays, foam, rubber,
lining, laces and reflective graphics. Each visible component has persistent
RenderMaterial, BaseColor and Roughness properties.

For Blender or another glTF-compatible renderer, import the GLB. Materials,
component names, normal vectors and placeholder colors are embedded.
The original GLB accompanies the static reconstruction; it is not regenerated
by edits to Cougar_Trail_Editable.FCStd. Use its separate STEP for this refinement.
CAD is millimetres and Z-up;
the GLB is metres and Y-up, with the axis conversion already applied.

For KeyShot or another CAD renderer, import the STEP with part names and colors
preserved. Assign final materials by the numbered group/material names.
The editable model's headless STEP uses native component names rather than
placeholder GUI colors; its JSON sidecar maps names to materials and labels.
Use export_cougar_step.py for subsequent STEP revisions; see Editing_guide.txt.
The EVA midsole is ONE continuous solid, not subdivided for a color gradient.
Apply the cyan-to-lime fade only as a rendering material.
Its heel flare, concave waist, rolled lasting lip and toe rocker are modeled
cross-sectional geometry, not a straight extruded wall or a painted groove.
Rubber shoulder inserts rise into matching cut pockets in the EVA.
Fabric panels are smooth continuous shells: there are no knit-relief solids,
fabric perforations or modeled lace tracer yarns. Add those appearances with
the visualization team's fabric and lace materials. Structural seam stitches
are retained as independently hideable geometry.

DESIGN AND SCALE
One shoe, X from heel to toe; -Y lateral/logo side, +Y medial side; Z up.
Nominal sole length: 300 mm. Nominal maximum sole width: about 120 mm.
Upper wall: 1.25 mm. Bonded overlays: approximately 0.5-0.95 mm.
Lace section: approximately 3.6 x 2.2 mm. Traction block height: 5.8 mm.
The overall envelope also includes protruding grips, bindings and overlays.

The model includes an open collar and lining, internal sockliner, separate tongue,
through-bored eyestays, eyelet rings, crisscross lacing and upper/lower bridges,
projected side supports and an outlined running-cat graphic,
wrapped toe protection, rear counter and stitched coral spine, sculpted sole
channels, separate rockered rubber carrier and alternating chevron traction lugs.
Only structural seam stitches are modeled; textile microstructure is not.

PANEL AND JOIN REFINEMENT
The quarter-to-vamp textile boundary is a shared curved cut with a 2.1 mm seam
binding. The upper has no arbitrary transverse material divisions.
The lateral support cage and medial chevrons use closed projected outlines with
variable width, rather than constant-width strips. Frame windows expose the
unperforated textile beneath; they are openings in the TPU, not fabric holes.
Bonded overlays have 0.42 mm exposed flanges and separately named joining lands.
First eyestays span two lace holes and have molded inset relief. A continuous
scalloped facing and a rounded throat end join the tongue to the vamp.
The toe guard has asymmetric wings and a 1 mm exposed bonding land.
The thin reflective transfer sits underneath the structural overlays.
These joining details are inferred from the images, not a factory construction
specification. PanelThickness and JoinType identify the intended construction.

EDITING / REBUILD
Use Cougar_Trail_Editable.FCStd for human adjustments; see Editing_guide.txt.
Its panel outlines, shared textile boundary, sole sections, lug profiles,
eyelets and lace paths drive linked features. It uses standard FreeCAD features
and bundled Draft paths, not an external custom feature proxy.
The reconstructed last, supporting surface stocks and some complex details
remain fixed reference geometry. It is not a fully constrained shoe-sizing model.

The original Cougar_Trail.FCStd contains static Part features by rendering material.
DesignParameters records nominal specifications; changing those properties alone
does not regenerate the shoe. The accompanying build_cougar.py is the editable,
script-driven construction source. Modify its station tables, surface definitions,
panel paths or lace layout, then rebuild and rerun the presentation macro.

From the project root on this Mac:

PYTHONPATH=/Applications/FreeCAD.app/Contents/Resources/lib \
  /Applications/FreeCAD.app/Contents/Resources/bin/python \
  examples/cougar-shoes/build_cougar.py

Then execute examples/cougar-shoes/present_cougar.FCMacro in FreeCAD to apply
native display colors and save twelve review views. STEP export is separate.
The build writes into this cad directory; preserve edited outputs before rebuilding.

ACCURACY LIMITS
Both supplied perspective concept images were used as visual references. There
is no dimensioned drawing, physical last or scan, so scale, cross-sections, hidden
surfaces, outsole layout and small details are inferred. The asymmetric overlay
language and color placement follow the references, but the model is a visual
reconstruction, not an exact reverse-engineered physical product.

This is a product-visualization asset, not manufacturing-released tooling.
It has no fit validation, shrink compensation, mold split, material simulation
or production tolerancing approval. Separate bonded components intentionally
overlap, and repeated stitch/lug solids remain compounds for material assignment.
No specific commercial material or validated physical properties are claimed.
